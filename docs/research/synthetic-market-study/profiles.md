# Synthetic stakeholder profiles — AI-generated simulation

Date: 16 October 2026 (Asia/Almaty). All ten names, biographies, ages, occupations, budgets, scores, health contexts and attitudes below are fictional, AI-generated simulation. No person was recruited or interviewed; any resemblance to a real person is coincidental. These profiles are hypothesis-generation devices, not empirical segmentation or a description of Kazakhstan’s population.

MedPredict is an educational diploma project at Astana IT University by Sanat Bogenbayev, Ali Baymen and Oralkhan Seilkhan. The attachment names M. K. Turarova as advisor; repository documentation still marks the advisor as unconfirmed, so supervision must be confirmed by the team. The existing React/TypeScript/Vite frontend is implemented. Java/Spring Boot and the model are ongoing work; ONNX is proposed, not verified integration. The prototype is not intended as a medical device or for diagnosis, treatment or clinical decisions; no clinical certification has been established in the inspected evidence.

## Scope and field-count resolution

The user confirmed the educational-prototype assumptions at Step 0. Primary audience: university students/researchers and educational evaluators (U01, U02, U10). U03 is an adjacent technical observer. U04–U09 deliberately explore boundary cases; their inclusion does not authorize patient deployment or establish a market. U01 is the representative **synthetic** persona and does not meet the Disciplined Entrepreneurship requirement for an actual, researched end user.

The attachment says “exactly 30” but enumerates 35 individual attributes. Each profile therefore has exactly 30 numbered rows. Five pairs retain both subattributes in one row: occupation + education (3), family + backstory (5), access + visit frequency (8), tracking habits + apps/wearables (20), and previous predictive-tool experience + initial stance (25). No listed detail is dropped. Assigned 1–10 scores are narrative constraints, not measurements. Budgets are fictional monthly total health/wellness outlay and must never be interpreted as willingness to pay.

Expected U01 lifecycle, all hypothetical: encounter the project in coursework → read purpose and limitations → access the existing demo without an account → enter fabricated examples → distinguish the fixed output from inference → inspect units/export → discuss the evidence with a supervisor → close the session. No discovery channel, retention or value claim has been observed.
