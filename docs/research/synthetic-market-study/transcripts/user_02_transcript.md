# U02 — Timur Beketov: synthetic interview

Date: 16 October 2026 (Asia/Almaty).

Timur Beketov and all interview people, roles, answers and quotations below are fictional AI-generated simulation. This is not a real interview or verified research, and it supplies no human evidence about demand, willingness to pay or usability. The dialogue is a scripted simulation rather than a recording or observation of a live interface.

MedPredict is an educational diploma project at Astana IT University by Sanat Bogenbayev, Ali Baymen and Oralkhan Seilkhan. M. K. Turarova is named as advisor in the attachment, but advisor confirmation remains outstanding in the repository. The React/TypeScript/Vite frontend is implemented; Java/Spring Boot and machine-learning work are ongoing; ONNX is proposed. The current demo returns a fixed example, not inference from submitted inputs.

MedPredict is not intended as a medical device or for diagnosis, treatment or clinical decisions. No clinical certification is established here. Human and expert research is still needed. These interviews cannot evaluate model accuracy, performance or Python–Java parity, and no such results are claimed. No actual personal measurements or patient data are requested or used.

Sources for this simulation: [U02 profile](../profiles-v2.md) and [interview script](../interview_script.md). Only U02's 30-field profile informed the fictional participant.
