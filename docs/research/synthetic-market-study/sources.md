# Source register and evidence boundaries

Date: 16 October 2026 (Asia/Almaty). This document supports an **AI-generated simulation**, not completed human research. All synthetic personas, interviews, quotes and findings are fictional and generate hypotheses only. MedPredict is an educational diploma project at Astana IT University by Sanat Bogenbayev, Ali Baymen and Oralkhan Seilkhan. The attachment names M. K. Turarova as advisor; repository confirmation remains outstanding. React/TypeScript/Vite frontend is implemented; Java/Spring Boot and ML work are ongoing, with ONNX proposed rather than verified. The prototype is not intended as a medical device or for diagnosis, treatment or clinical decisions; no clinical certification is established in the inspected evidence. Real-user and expert validation remain necessary.

## Evidence categories

- **Repository evidence:** statements about inspected local artifacts, not an independent audit of every behavior or a current backend deployment.
- **External primary source:** a public framework, research abstract or official product page inspected for limited context. Source claims remain scoped to their stated setting.
- **AI-generated simulation:** invented profiles/dialogues and interpretations of those dialogues. They supply no empirical customer, clinical, accuracy, privacy or performance evidence.
- **Proposal:** future research, interview decision rules and recommendations. Nothing in this category has been executed or approved by the university merely because it is written here.

## Inference limits

E02/E03 do not establish that ten wholly invented personas predict real MedPredict users. Separate agents still share a model family and supplied framing. Persona traits and the script partly determine the resulting agreement; cross-transcript counts measure this generated corpus only. Age, occupation, location and assigned scores cannot be used to make demographic or cultural generalizations.

External source checks are targeted context checks, **not a completed systematic or academic literature review**. No market size, conversion, adoption rate, representative demand, clinical efficacy, certification, legal classification or willingness-to-pay estimate has been established. Product pages may change; no current price is asserted. Real research remains required even if every simulated answer appears coherent.
