# Revised synthetic profiles v2 — AI-generated simulation

Date: 16 October 2026 (Asia/Almaty). This is the user-requested revised mix, superseding [profiles.md](profiles.md) for all interviews and synthesis. The original is preserved. All names, biographies, ages, budgets, 1–10 scores, health contexts and attitudes are fictional, AI-generated simulation; none are recruited people or validated target customers. Coincidental resemblance does not identify a real person. Scores and financial amounts are invented narrative constraints, not population measurements or willingness-to-pay evidence.

MedPredict is an educational diploma project at Astana IT University by Sanat Bogenbayev, Ali Baymen and Oralkhan Seilkhan. M. K. Turarova is named as advisor in the attachment but remains unconfirmed in the repository. The existing React/TypeScript/Vite frontend is implemented; Java/Spring Boot and ML work are ongoing, and ONNX is proposed rather than verified integration. The prototype is not intended as a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment or clinical decisions. No clinical certification is established by inspected evidence. AI-generated interviews and findings cannot replace real-user research or clinical/expert consultation.

## User-approved distribution and 30-field schema

U01–U02: university student and medical AI research audience. U03–U04: adults interested in monitoring indicators. U05: healthcare professional interested in data analysis. U06: medical researcher evaluating prediction accuracy. U07: health-technology early adopter. U08: older adult with limited digital literacy. U09: privacy-conscious skeptic. U10: lecturer evaluating the academic prototype. All ten are exploratory personas. Breadth tests assumptions and project boundaries; it does not establish a beachhead market or authorize patient deployment.

Exactly 30 numbered characteristics per profile preserve all 35 details enumerated in the original attachment. Paired fields are occupation/education (3), family/backstory (5), healthcare access/visit frequency (8), tracking habits/apps (20), and previous predictive-tool experience/initial stance (25). U01 is only a provisional synthetic representative; an actual researched persona remains missing under Disciplined Entrepreneurship. Names, ages and traits must not be interpreted as demographic causes of attitudes.

The confirmed educational lifecycle remains a hypothesis: discover through coursework → inspect purpose/limitations → access existing demo → enter fabricated input → distinguish fixed output from inference → examine units and optional PDF → discuss with a supervisor → end session. Potential patient-monitoring scenarios are explored only for relevance and misinterpretation risks.
